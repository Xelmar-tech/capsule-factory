# Humanizing Text

Remove AI patterns and add human voice.

## AI Patterns to Remove

### Inflated Significance
- "stands as a testament"
- "pivotal moment"
- "evolving landscape"
- "indelible mark"

**Fix**: Be direct. "The company launched in 2019" not "The company's launch marked a pivotal moment."

### Superficial -ing Analyses
- "highlighting," "ensuring," "reflecting," "symbolizing"

**Fix**: State facts directly. "The building is blue" not "The building's color highlights the sky."

### Promotional Language
- "boasts," "vibrant," "rich," "breathtaking," "stunning"

**Fix**: Neutral tone. "The town has a market" not "The town boasts a vibrant market."

### Vague Attributions
- "Experts argue," "Industry reports," "Some critics"

**Fix**: Name sources. "In a 2024 interview, Jane said..."

### AI Vocabulary
- "delve," "leverage," "robust," "seamless," "holistic"

**Fix**: Use simpler words. "Explore" not "delve." "Use" not "leverage."

## Adding Human Voice

### Have Opinions
"I don't know how to feel about this" > neutrally listing pros and cons

### Vary Rhythm
Short sentences. Then longer ones that take their time.

### Acknowledge Complexity
"This is impressive but also kind of unsettling"

### Use "I" When Appropriate
"I keep coming back to..." signals a real person.

### Let Mess In
Tangents, asides, half-formed thoughts are human.

## Workflow

1. **Scan** for AI patterns
2. **Rewrite** problematic sections
3. **Add** voice and personality
4. **Verify** it sounds like a person wrote it

## Example

Before:
> The experiment produced interesting results. The agents generated 3 million lines of code. Some developers were impressed while others were skeptical. The implications remain unclear.

After:
> I genuinely don't know how to feel about this one. 3 million lines of code, generated while the humans presumably slept. Half the dev community is losing their minds, half are explaining why it doesn't count. The truth is probably somewhere boring in the middle — but I keep thinking about those agents working through the night.
